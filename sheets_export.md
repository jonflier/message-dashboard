## Teamwork Illustration #5
**Category**: Teamwork | **Source**: James E. Lukaszewski, management and communication consultant

A farmer, while sitting on his porch, noticed a highway department truck pulling over to the shoulder of the road. A man got out, dug a sizeable hole in the ditch, and got back into the vehicle. A few minutes later, the other occupant of the truck got out, filled up the hole, tamped the dirt, and returned to the truck.

Then the men drove forward on the shoulder about 50 yards and repeated the process – digging, waiting, refilling. After a half-dozen repetitions, the farmer sauntered over to them. “What are you doing?” he asked.  “We’re on a highway beautification project,” the driver said. “And the guy who plants the trees is home sick today.”

## Unity Illustration #6
**Category**: Unity | **Source**: John C. Maxwell, "Developing the Leaders Around You"

The story goes that there was a man walking down a residential street when he noticed another man trying to move his washing machine. The first man volunteered to help, to which the second man was overjoyed. After some fruitless effort, the two looked at each other with exhaustion. The first man said, "How are we ever going to get this washing machine inside of here." Surprised, the second man said, "Inside? I thought we were trying to move it outside?"
